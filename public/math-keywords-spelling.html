Math Keywords & Spelling

This activity builds the language pupils repeatedly meet in Singapore P1–P3 Mathematics.

  1. Select the Primary level and number of questions.
  2. Each item gives a mathematical visual clue and says the word aloud.
  3. The pupil types the spelling. A correct first checked answer earns 1 mark.
  4. If the first answer is wrong, the correct spelling is shown for self-correction. Later retries remain practice and do not replace the first-attempt score.
  5. The audio may be replayed because hearing the target word is part of the spelling task; replaying it does not count as help.

Important: the visuals teach relationships and meanings. They do not teach a rigid “keyword = operation” rule.
