Math Keywords & Spelling

This topic has two deliberately different modes.

  1. Live engine = presentation teaching mode. The Math word is shown in large type with its visual. Use Previous word, Hear word, Hide word for quick retrieval, Spelling clue to highlight a useful spelling chunk, and Next word. There are no answer blanks, marks or spelling checks in the live engine.
  2. Downloaded / SLS activity = pupil spelling test. Pupils hear the target and type the spelling.
  3. Hint: Show some letters gives a partial spelling pattern with missing letters, for example a _ t _ g _ t h _ r. It does not reveal the full answer.
  4. Using the letter hint counts as help, so that question becomes assisted practice and does not earn a mark. A correct first checked spelling without a hint earns 1 mark.
  5. Replaying the audio does not count as help because hearing the target word is part of a spelling test.

Important: the visuals teach relationships and meanings. They do not teach a rigid “keyword = operation” rule.
