Teacher guide

P1 · 2D shapes

Name and describe rectangles, squares, triangles, circles, half circles and quarter circles. Explicitly teach vertex for one corner and vertices for more than one. Pupils count straight sides and vertices, recognise component shapes, form figures from shapes, and copy figures on a dot grid. In the building activity, pupils select pieces to reproduce the target. In the copy activity, they tap the same vertices on the blank grid; starting at any vertex and drawing clockwise or anticlockwise is accepted.

P2 · Patterns and 3D shapes

Complete patterns involving shape, size, colour and orientation. Identify, name, describe and classify cubes, cuboids, cones, cylinders and spheres.

P3 · Angles and lines

Recognise right angles and compare other angles with a right angle. Recognise and actually draw parallel and perpendicular lines. In the drawing task, a black reference line and a gold point are given. Pupils tap a second grid point to create the blue line; the engine checks the mathematical relationship, not a pre-drawn Yes/No choice.

Sorter approach: inspired by your P3 Angle Dock and Line Dock activities. Pupils classify the visual first; no degree or answer label is printed on the object. Angle sizes and line orientations vary so pupils do not rely on position.
