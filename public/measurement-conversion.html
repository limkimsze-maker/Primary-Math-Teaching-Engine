Teacher guide

Teaching sequence

This engine follows the gradual support idea used in the Measurement conversion practices: Easy shows helper steps, Guided shows one conversion line, and Independent removes the scaffold.

Conversion relationships

  • 1 km = 1000 m
  • 1 m = 100 cm
  • 1 kg = 1000 g
  • 1 ℓ = 1000 ml

Suggested language

When changing from a larger unit to a smaller unit, ask pupils how many small units make one large unit. For mixed units, convert the large-unit part first, then add the remaining small units. In the reverse direction, find complete groups of 1000 (or 100 for m and cm); the remainder stays in the smaller unit.

Watch for

Common errors include changing the unit label without changing the number, adding 1000 instead of multiplying, and losing the zero in examples such as 3 kg 50 g = 3050 g.
