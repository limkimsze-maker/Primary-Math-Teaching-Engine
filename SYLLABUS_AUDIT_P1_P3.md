# P1–P3 MOE Mathematics Syllabus Coverage Audit

Audit date: 22 September 2026

Official reference: Singapore MOE, **2021 Primary Mathematics Syllabus P1–P6, updated October 2025**, especially pages 31–36 for Primary 1 to Primary 3:
https://www.moe.gov.sg/-/media/files/primary/2021-primary-mathematics-syllabus-p1-to-p6-updated-october-2025.pdf

This audit compares the official P1–P3 content objectives with the current Primary Maths Studio main branch.

## Status key

- ✅ **Covered** — an explicit current activity teaches or practises the objective.
- 🟡 **Partial** — related material exists, but at least one required element is not directly practised or assessed.
- ❌ **Missing** — no direct current activity was found for that objective.

## Bar-model curriculum audit — P1 to P3 (28 Sep 2026)

The Bar Models engine was reviewed against the Primary Mathematics syllabus progression and Singapore model-method structures.

- **P1:** retain pictorial addition, subtraction and simple additive comparison. Do not introduce formal multiplicative-comparison unit models here.
- **P2:** additive part–whole and additive comparison remain core. The engine now includes the two missing inverse comparison forms (find the larger quantity; find the smaller quantity). P2 multiplication/division is kept to the 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 table families, with three equal-group pictorial/unit forms: given each + groups → total; given total + groups → each; given total + each → number of groups.
- **P3:** this is the main level for formal multiplication/division part–whole and comparison models. The engine now includes all three equal-unit inverse forms and multiplicative comparison using “twice as many” and “n times as many”.
- **P3 multiplicative comparison inverse family:** find larger from smaller + multiplier; find smaller from larger + multiplier; find combined total; find difference; given combined total + multiplier find either quantity; given difference + multiplier find either quantity; given both quantities find the multiplier.
- **Two-step P3 model use:** total-known and difference-known multiplicative comparisons deliberately require pupils to find 1 unit before the requested quantity. These support the P3 expectation of solving up to 2-step word problems involving the four operations.
- **Not separate base-model types:** chained/multi-person questions are combinations of the same equal-unit and comparison structures, so they belong in P3 multi-step word-problem practice rather than requiring a new mathematical model family.
- **Beyond P3:** fraction/ratio/percentage unit models and complex before–after/transfer models should not be pulled down into this P1–P3 Bar Models strand merely for completeness.

## Current high-priority gaps

To make the Studio defensibly **full P1–P3 syllabus coverage**, the clearest missing or incomplete areas are:

1. **P1:** ordinal numbers; adding more than two 1-digit numbers; a.m./p.m.; stronger P1 mental calculation; direct number-word writing; explicit addition/subtraction related facts.
2. **P2:** odd/even numbers; P2 mental addition/subtraction; explicit multiplication/division related facts; mental multiplication/division fluency; compare/order money amounts; convert hours/minutes ↔ minutes.
3. **P3:** seconds; full start/finish/duration inverse family; 24-hour clock; mental multiplication/division fluency; explicit square area/perimeter; direct number-word writing; a clearer km measurement context.

## Existing activities that are beyond the minimum content list

The Studio also contains useful extensions and pedagogical supports that are not separate MOE content objectives, including:

- P3 More Word Problems / Monster Quest
- money word-problem model selection
- area of rectilinear figures
- area of figures with half-squares
- error analysis
- explanation/reasoning activities
- P3 mental-sum strategy lessons
- progressive Easy → Guided → Independent scaffolds

These strengthen problem solving and conceptual understanding, but they do not replace a missing syllabus objective above.

## Conclusion

The Studio is **broadly comprehensive, especially for P3, but it is not yet full P1–P3 syllabus coverage**. The most efficient path is to add only the missing/partial objectives listed above rather than create new versions of already-covered topics.
